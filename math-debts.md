# Math debts

Where my math falls off — tracked **as I hit it**, not front-loaded.

My rule: start from where I'm comfortable and go down rabbit holes from there.
I'm deliberately **not** building foundations ground-up first — if I do that I
won't start at all. A debt gets logged the moment a real thing (an experiment,
a paper) needs math I don't have, and it's paid down only as far as that thing
requires.
